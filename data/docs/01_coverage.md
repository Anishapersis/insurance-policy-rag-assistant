# Northwind Auto Policy: Coverage Summary

> Sample policy wording written for this demo. Northwind Insurance is a fictional insurer, and this document is not a real insurance contract.

## Third-Party Liability
Third-party liability covers you if you injure or kill someone, or damage their property, while using your insured vehicle. The minimum limit in Ontario is $200,000, but this policy provides a standard limit of $1,000,000. Optional limits of $2,000,000 are available for an additional premium. Liability coverage also pays the legal costs of defending you against a covered lawsuit, and these costs do not reduce your limit.

## Direct Compensation - Property Damage (DCPD)
DCPD pays for damage to your vehicle and its contents when another driver is at fault for a collision in Ontario. The claim is made directly to Northwind rather than to the other driver's insurer. If you are 0% at fault, no deductible applies. If you are partly at fault, you are paid in proportion to the other driver's share of fault.

## Collision or Upset
Collision coverage pays to repair or replace your vehicle when you are at fault, or when the at-fault party cannot be identified, including hitting an object or the vehicle rolling over. The standard deductible for collision coverage is $1,000.

## Comprehensive
Comprehensive coverage protects against loss or damage from causes other than collision, including fire, theft, vandalism, falling or flying objects, windstorm, hail, flood and contact with animals such as a deer. The standard deductible for comprehensive coverage is $500. Glass-only claims for a cracked windshield have a reduced deductible of $250.

## Uninsured Automobile
Uninsured automobile coverage protects you and your passengers if you are injured or killed by an uninsured or unidentified driver (a hit-and-run). It also covers damage to your vehicle caused by an identified uninsured driver.

## Optional Coverage: Rental Vehicle (OPCF 20)
If your vehicle cannot be driven because of a covered loss, this endorsement pays for a rental vehicle or transportation costs up to $70 per day, to a maximum of $2,100 per claim.

## Optional Coverage: Waiver of Depreciation (OPCF 43)
For vehicles under 24 months old, this endorsement waives depreciation when calculating a total-loss settlement, so you are paid the original purchase price rather than the current market value.
