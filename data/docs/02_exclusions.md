# Northwind Auto Policy: Exclusions and Conditions

> Sample policy wording written for this demo (fictional insurer).

## General Exclusions
This policy does not cover loss or damage that occurs while the vehicle is:
- being used to carry paying passengers, including ride-sharing services such as Uber or Lyft, unless the ride-sharing endorsement (OPCF 49) is purchased;
- being used in a race or speed test;
- driven by a person who is not authorized by law to drive, including a driver whose licence is suspended;
- rented or leased to another person.

## Wear and Tear
Mechanical breakdown, rust, corrosion, tire damage caused by road conditions alone, and normal wear and tear are not covered under any section of the policy.

## Intoxication
If the driver is impaired by alcohol or drugs, or refuses a breath or blood test, coverage for damage to your own vehicle under Collision and Comprehensive is void for that loss. Third-party liability and accident benefits still apply to protect injured third parties, but Northwind may seek to recover amounts paid from the driver.

## Misrepresentation
If you knowingly give false information on your application or in a claim, Northwind may deny the claim and cancel the policy. Examples include failing to disclose a principal driver, misrepresenting where the vehicle is garaged, or inflating the value of a loss.

## Driving Outside Canada
Coverage applies in Canada and the United States, and on vessels travelling between ports in those countries. Physical damage coverage does not apply in Mexico or any other country.
