# Northwind Auto Policy: How to Make a Claim

> Sample policy wording written for this demo (fictional insurer).

## Reporting a Loss
You must report any accident or loss to Northwind within 7 days, or as soon as reasonably possible. Claims can be reported 24/7 through the mobile app or by calling the claims line. If anyone is injured, if the total damage appears to exceed $2,000, or if a criminal act such as theft or vandalism is involved, you must also report the incident to the police.

## Collision Reporting Centres
In parts of Ontario, collisions without injuries are reported to a Collision Reporting Centre within 24 hours rather than at the scene. Bring your driver's licence, ownership, insurance card and the vehicle itself to the centre.

## Documents You Will Need
- the police report or Collision Reporting Centre report number;
- photos of the damage and of the scene;
- names, licence numbers and insurance details of other drivers;
- contact details of any witnesses;
- repair estimates or receipts, if available.

## Proof of Loss
For theft and total-loss claims, you must submit a signed Proof of Loss form within 90 days of the loss. Payment is normally issued within 30 days after Northwind receives the completed Proof of Loss and agrees on the amount.

## Choosing a Repair Shop
You may choose any licensed repair facility. Northwind's preferred shops offer a lifetime guarantee on workmanship for as long as you own the vehicle. An appraiser may inspect the damage before repairs begin.

## Disputes and Appraisal
If you disagree with the amount offered for your vehicle, either party may ask for an appraisal. Each side appoints an appraiser, and the two appraisers choose an umpire. A decision agreed by any two of the three is binding on the amount of the loss.

## Fraud Investigations
Northwind's Special Investigations Unit (SIU) reviews claims that show signs of possible fraud, such as staged collisions, inflated repair invoices or inconsistent statements. A referral to the SIU does not mean a claim will be denied; it means additional documentation may be requested.
