# Northwind Auto Policy: Statutory Accident Benefits

> Sample policy wording written for this demo (fictional insurer). Amounts reflect a simplified illustration of standard Ontario benefits.

## Who Is Covered
Accident benefits are paid to you, your passengers and pedestrians injured by your vehicle, regardless of who caused the accident.

## Income Replacement
If you cannot work because of the accident, income replacement pays 70% of your gross income, up to a maximum of $400 per week. There is a one-week waiting period before payments begin. Optional coverage can increase the maximum to $600, $800 or $1,000 per week.

## Medical, Rehabilitation and Attendant Care
For non-catastrophic injuries, the combined limit for medical, rehabilitation and attendant care is $65,000. Minor injuries such as sprains, strains and whiplash fall under the Minor Injury Guideline, which has a limit of $3,500. For catastrophic injuries, the combined limit is $1,000,000.

## Caregiver and Housekeeping Benefits
Caregiver and housekeeping benefits are available only for catastrophic injuries under the standard policy. They can be added for all injuries through optional coverage.

## Death and Funeral Benefits
A death benefit of $25,000 is paid to an eligible spouse, and $10,000 to each dependant. Funeral expenses are reimbursed up to $6,000.

## Applying for Benefits
You must notify Northwind within 7 days of the accident that you intend to apply for accident benefits, and submit the application form (OCF-1) within 30 days of receiving it.
