# Northwind Auto Policy: Premiums, Billing and FAQ

> Sample policy wording written for this demo (fictional insurer).

## Paying Your Premium
Premiums can be paid annually or in 12 monthly instalments. Monthly payments carry a service charge of 3% of the annual premium. If a payment is missed, Northwind sends a notice and the policy may be cancelled 15 days after the notice is delivered by registered mail.

## Discounts
- Multi-vehicle discount: 10% when two or more vehicles are insured with Northwind.
- Winter tire discount: 5% when four certified winter tires are installed from December 1 to March 31.
- Conviction-free discount: 5% for drivers with no traffic convictions in the past 3 years.
- Telematics programme: up to 25% based on driving behaviour measured by the mobile app.

## Will a Claim Increase My Premium?
An at-fault claim may increase your premium at renewal. Not-at-fault claims, including DCPD claims where you are 0% at fault, will not increase your premium. Accident forgiveness, available after 6 consecutive claim-free years, protects your rate after your first at-fault accident.

## Adding a Driver
You must tell Northwind within 30 days when a new driver in your household gets a licence, or when someone begins driving your vehicle regularly.

## Cancelling Your Policy
You can cancel at any time. If you cancel, the unused premium is refunded on a short-rate basis, which includes an administrative charge. If Northwind cancels, the refund is calculated pro rata, with no penalty.
